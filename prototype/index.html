What this prototype demonstrates

The core loop

Join a business → earn points at checkout → redeem a reward via QR/PIN — end to end, in both the customer app and the staff POS view.

Two identity realms

One customer account with independent balances at multiple businesses, vs. tenant staff scoped to a single business — the platform's foundational design decision.

Every MVP surface

30 customer screens, 17 business-portal screens, 14 admin-portal screens — with loading, empty, error and success states throughout.

Eksabli — built on ABP Framework + Angular + Flutter. This prototype uses static demo data; no network calls are made.
